Interlibrary loans

Borrowing rules and policy

To borrow, search the catalog, open a title another library holds, and choose Request to borrow (school libraries, 1-day loan) or Borrow this bin (Library Hubs, 1 month, up to 2 bins). The lending library approves the request, marks the books as released, and marks them returned when they come back.
